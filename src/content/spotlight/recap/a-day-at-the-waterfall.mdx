On 7 November 2026, the Strathmore Computing and Engineering Students Association (SCESA) took its members to Njine Kabia for a team-building day. The day was promoted alongside the Strathmore Data Community, the Strathmore University Engineering Students Association, and the AWS Student Builder Group.

&#x20;The day began on the road, with a bus full of cameras and laughter. The group then walked a muddy trail lined with banana plants. Umbrellas went up through a light drizzle, but nobody minded, and the walk was as much about getting to know each other as about the destination.&#x20;

Along the way, teams worked through squat-and-stand challenges, a group dance, and a bamboo-and-rope obstacle course. A few members also took a turn on a swing near the trees.&#x20;

The highlight was the waterfall. Participants waded into the pool below the falls, lined up on the wet rocks, and took turns under the spray. Several people braved the cold water together.&#x20;

The afternoon ended with a shared meal. Stories from the trail were retold, and the conversation continued long after the last game.&#x20;

> ###### SCESA thanks everyone who made the day possible, along with the Strathmore Data Community, the Strathmore University Engineering Students Association, and the AWS Student Builder Group for their support.
