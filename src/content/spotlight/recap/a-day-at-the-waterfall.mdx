The SCESA's team-building day took place on 7 November 2026 at **Njine Kabia**, running from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. The event was promoted alongside the **Strathmore Data Community**, the **Strathmore University Engineering Students Association**, and the **AWS Student Builder Group**.

The day started on the road. Participants crowded into a bus, cameras out, with plenty of laughter as the group settled in for the trip.

Once off the bus, the group set out on foot along a muddy trail lined with banana plants. Umbrellas came out as a light drizzle fell, but nobody seemed to mind. The walk was as much about chatting and getting to know each other as about the destination.

Along the way there were games that got everyone moving. Teams worked through squat-and-stand challenges, a group dance routine, and a few playful obstacles built from bamboo poles and rope, with a few members stepping up to take a turn at a swing near the trees.

> A day filled with adventure, laughter, teamwork and unforgettable memories! 🥳🌿 SCESA Team Building 2026 was definitely one for the books. Thank you to everyone who made it special! 💛

The highlight was the waterfall. The group waded into the pool below the falls, lining up on the wet rocks and taking turns under the spray. Several people braved the cold water together, and the mood stayed light throughout.

As the afternoon wore on, the group gathered to eat and unwind. Plates were passed around, stories from the trail got retold, and the banter kept coming long after the last of the games had ended. Some people lingered over their food while others wandered back to the spot by the falls, and the easy laughter that had carried the day stayed with everyone right up to the end.

#### Thank you to everyone who made the day what it was. 💛
