On Friday 19 June 2026, SCESA's name was read out at the Strathmore Clubs and Sports Gala, and we went up to the stage. Then it happened again. By the end of the night we had been called up four times.

The gala is the one evening when the whole campus gathers to look back on the year's student initiatives and find out how it really went. For us, it ended with four awards for the 2025/26 academic year.

## What we won

### Best Organised Event 2025: the AI Students Summit

The summit brought students, researchers and tech professionals into one room to dig into emerging AI frameworks and the career paths around them. This award is for the work behind it: the planning, the speakers and the logistics. If you attended, you saw that planning pay off.

### Most Rewarding Club 2025

This one is about what members get out of SCESA. In hands-on technical workshops, peer mentorship, hackathons and project demos, members pick up skills, meet people and find opportunities.

### Best Club Per Category 2025: IT, Innovation, and Research Clubs

Among the clubs doing the kind of work closest to ours, SCESA came out on top.

### Collaborations and Partnerships 2025

Over the past year we partnered with industry, with external developer communities and with student organisations across faculties. This award is for that work.

## Who made it happen

Trophies get built in quiet computer labs, on late-night planning calls, and in lecture halls where members stayed behind to stack chairs and test the AV.

The outgoing 2025/26 executive board led that work:

- Elvina Rioba (President)
- Maryanne Kariuki (Vice-president)
- Nathan Githinji Rugo (Secretary)
- Stacy Mwangi (Director of Communications)
- Ravine Riang'a (Membership Coordinator)
- Victor Mwakisagu (Project Manager)
- Francis Wainaina (Financial Executive)

They worked alongside their technical sub-community leads, and they set up how SCESA runs in a way that should outlast their term.

We had help from beyond the association too. Paul Ochieng, Dean of Students, gave us mentorship and guidance. Michael Owiti, Manager of Student Affairs, made space for our activities and backed us with operational support. The Strathmore University Student Council supported us as well.

And to our members: whether you led a study session, mentored a peer, asked a question at a summit or gave up a weekend to run logistics, these awards are yours. SCESA belongs to you.

## What's next

The incoming board for 2026/27 says stepping into this is humbling, and that it has them fired up.
